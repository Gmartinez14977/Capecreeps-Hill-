# Capecrepps-Hill-
Theme and Storyline 
Theme: 
Capecrepps Hill is a haunted mansion filled with mysterious and cursed objects. The player must explore the mansion while avoiding the ghost of its former owner, Mordecai. 

Storyline: 
The player enters the abandoned Capecrepps Hill mansion and becomes trapped inside by Mordecai, the ghost of the mansion's former owner. 
To escape, the player must explore the mansion and collect eight cursed objects hidden throughout the rooms. The player must collect the Old Spell Book, Silver Knife, Cursed Goblet, Ancient Key, Haunted Doll, Broken Mirror, Magic Cape,
and Curse Stone before entering the Wine Cellar, where Mordecai is waiting. If the player collects all eight items before encountering Mordecai, they escape the mansion and win the game. 
If the player enters the Wine Cellar before collecting all eight items, Mordecai catches the player and the game ends.
